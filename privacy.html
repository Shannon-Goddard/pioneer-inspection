Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

1. Overview

Pioneer Inspection LLC ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website and how we use it.

The short version: we do not track you, we do not use cookies, and we do not sell your information. The only data we collect is what you voluntarily submit through our contact form.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. When you fill out the contact form on our website, you may provide:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (optional)
  • A message describing your project or inquiry

We do not collect any information automatically. There are no cookies, no tracking pixels, no analytics scripts, and no third-party advertising on this site.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information you submit through our contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry. We will not:

  • Sell or rent your information to anyone
  • Share your information with third parties except as needed to respond to you
  • Add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent

4. Contact Form & Formspree

Our contact form is processed through Formspree, a third-party form service. When you submit the form, your message is transmitted to Formspree's servers and forwarded to our email inbox. Formspree's own privacy policy applies to that transmission and can be found at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

5. Cookies

This website does not use cookies of any kind.

6. Analytics

This website does not currently use any analytics tools (such as Google Analytics). If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated to reflect it.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites such as Calendly (for scheduling) and LinkedIn. Once you leave our site, this privacy policy no longer applies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.
