NETTCP Paving Inspections

Transmission and distribution piping systems are often installed under roadways and sidewalks. This means part of the trench restoration may require pavement and curbing to be restored as well. This critical step, if disregarded, can leave customers and local residents feeling dissatisfied which may then lead to complaints and issues with town, city, and state personnel.

Working with Pioneer Inspection means less headaches and better quality.

Pioneer Inspection can be there at the final stages of your piping project to review contractor paving practices and provide recommendations to prevent non-compliance with the requirements of state and local municipalities. We can visit the construction sites long after the crews are gone to verify that your latest project is something you can feel proud of.

Our sense of pride goes way beyond simply getting the pipe in the ground. We treat the communities we work in the same as the communities we live in.
